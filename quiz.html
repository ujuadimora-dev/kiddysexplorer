How to Win a Prize

  1. Register your child for the Kiddysexplorer Quiz.
  2. Choose the correct quiz group based on your child’s age.
  3. Your child will take Section 1 of the quiz.
  4. If your child scores 80% or above, they can proceed to Section 2.
  5. If your child scores 80% or above in Section 2, they will wait for the live one-on-one session with the tutor.
  6. During the live session, your child will compete with other children in the same age group.
  7. Prize winners will be selected based on performance and final position.
  8. If your child scores below 80%, they should go back and watch Kiddysexplorer YouTube learning videos.
  9. After learning, your child can wait for the next quiz session and try again.
