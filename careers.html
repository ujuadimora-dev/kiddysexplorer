Who We Are Looking For

Kiddysexplorer needs passionate people in teaching, design, video production, music, and creative technology.

Teachers

Online teachers, preschool teachers, primary teachers, and subject specialists.

Designers

Graphic designers, illustrators, character designers, and thumbnail designers.

Video Creators

Video editors, motion designers, 2D/3D animators, and children’s video creators.

3D Artists

Blender artists, modelers, scene designers, and animation specialists.

Music & Voice

Children’s singers, voice actors, sound designers, and song writers.

AI Creators

AI video creators using tools like Runway, PixVerse, Kling, Veo, Suno, and ElevenLabs.

Specially for Teachers

Calling Passionate Teachers!

We are looking for enthusiastic educators who love working with children and want to make learning fun, creative, and meaningful.

Who Can Apply?

  • Nursery and primary school teachers
  • Preschool teachers
  • Subject specialists
  • Mathematics teachers
  • English language teachers
  • Science teachers
  • Storytellers and music teachers
  • Art, craft, and creativity teachers
  • Teachers with passion for children

Minimum Requirements

  • Good communication skills
  • Good spoken English
  • Laptop or desktop computer
  • Stable internet connection
  • Webcam and quiet space
  • Positive and child-friendly attitude
  • Reliable and punctual
  • Teaching experience is an advantage
Creative & Design Opportunities

Creative Designers & Digital Artists!

Are you passionate about creating engaging educational content for children? We're looking for talented designers, illustrators, animators, video editors, AI creators, and digital artists who can bring stories, songs, and learning experiences to life through creativity and innovation.

Who Can Apply?

  • Graphic Designers
  • Children's Book Illustrators
  • Character Designers
  • UI/UX Designers
  • Motion Graphics Designers
  • 2D Animators
  • 3D Artists (Blender, Maya, Cinema 4D)
  • Video Editors
  • Thumbnail Designers

Minimum Requirements

  • Strong creativity and attention to detail
  • Experience with one or more professional design tools
  • Laptop or desktop computer
  • Stable internet connection
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Ability to meet project deadlines
  • Portfolio of previous work (preferred)
  • Willingness to learn new tools and techniques
